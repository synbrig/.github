## Who we are

Synbrig is a small, founder-led engineering studio based in Denver, Colorado, working with businesses in the United States, United Kingdom, Australia and the Philippines.

We design, build and look after the software a business runs on: the website customers judge you by, the store that takes the payment, the internal tool that replaces the spreadsheet, and the data pipelines behind your reporting. One team, one point of contact, and no finger-pointing between a designer, a developer and a hosting company.

## How a project runs

```text
01  We talk          A call, not a form. A written summary of what we heard.
02  Fixed quote      Scope, timeline and price in writing before any work starts.
03  Design first     Real designs for every page, desktop and mobile, before building.
04  Build in view    A private staging link and a named engineer. No weeks of silence.
05  Launch properly  Domain, hosting, analytics, search setup, tested on real devices.
06  Look after it    A care plan, or a clean handover. Your call.
```

---

## Four things we put in writing

- **Fixed scope, no surprise invoices.** Written scope, timeline and price before any code is written.
- **You own everything.** Code, domain, hosting and analytics in your name. No vendor lock-in.
- **Direct access to the engineers.** You talk to the people building your product, not an account manager.
- **We say no when it's the right answer.** If something adds cost without real value, we'll tell you.
